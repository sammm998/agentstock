# Compliance checklist (Sweden / EU first)

This is an engineering checklist, **not legal advice**. Nothing here means Hustera is compliant; items need review by qualified counsel and providers.

| Area | Built in Milestone A | Still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GDPR — access & portability | JSON export of all personal data (`/api/account/export`) | Privacy policy, records of processing, DPA with hosting providers |
| GDPR — erasure | Account deletion with cascade + anonymisation | Backup retention policy |
| GDPR — minimisation | Email, password hash and profile only; no tracking scripts | Cookie/ePrivacy review once analytics are added |
| DSA — notice & action | Reports on all content types, moderation queue, logged decisions, user notification, appeals | Statement of reasons format, transparency reporting, trusted flaggers, point of contact |
| Consumer / marketing law | No income promises in product copy; guidelines ban them | Review of marketing materials |
| Sparks / payment & e-money law, MiCA | Sparks are non-cash, non-transferable, non-purchasable, non-redeemable; separate ledger; terms published | Legal review before any transferability, purchase, gifting or merchant acceptance |
| Hustera Card | Concept only, labelled | Licensed issuer, KYC/AML, safeguarding — Milestone I |
| Pooled community investment | Not built | Specialist authorisation (crowdfunding / AIF) — Milestone I |
| Age | — | Minimum-age policy and checks |
| Trademark | — | Clearance for "Hustera" name, domain and app stores |
